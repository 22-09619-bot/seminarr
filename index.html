Seminar 01 Face-to-face seminar

UplEEft - Empowering the Future, Human and Technical Skills One Engineer

DateFebruary 20, 2026
Time1:00 PM - 4:00 PM
Venue / PlatformAmphitheater, Albert Einstein Building, Batangas State University - Alangilan Campus

Overview

The seminar aimed to strengthen the human and technical competencies of future engineers. It featured discussions on SCADA, career advancement, personal growth, and the Renewable Energy Act. A key session was presented by Engr. Ian Medwin E. Dimaano, who explained the Renewable Energy Act of 2008, net metering, solar power systems, grid interconnection, and the necessary requirements for renewable energy applications.

Topics Discussed

  • SCADA
  • Career advancement
  • Personal growth and personal development
  • Renewable Energy Act
  • Net metering and solar power systems
  • Grid interconnection and renewable energy application requirements

Key Takeaway

Through this seminar, I realized that electrical engineers need to be knowledgeable not only in technical concepts, but also in government regulations, safety procedures, proper documentation, and industry standards. The event helped connect classroom learning with actual responsibilities and practices in the engineering field.

Final Reflection

Overall Learning Reflection

Overall, the seminars and colloquia provided me with valuable knowledge and insights into the different fields of electrical engineering. The events covered a wide range of topics, including renewable energy, automation, electrical design, energy management, research innovation, and community-based engineering solutions. Through these discussions and presentations, I gained a deeper understanding of how engineering concepts are applied in real-world situations to solve practical problems and improve systems.

The seminars also helped me realize that being an effective electrical engineer requires more than technical knowledge alone. Engineers must also possess critical thinking, adaptability, communication skills, professionalism, and resilience in order to meet the challenges of the industry. The presentations from professionals, researchers, and fellow students inspired me to appreciate the importance of continuous learning, innovation, and research in the advancement of engineering practice.

Most importantly, these events motivated me to become more responsible, creative, and socially aware as a future engineer. They showed how electrical engineering can contribute to sustainability, renewable energy development, automation, public service, and community improvement. Overall, the experience strengthened my passion for the field and encouraged me to pursue engineering not only as a profession, but also as a way to contribute positively to society.

Technical awareness

I gained deeper knowledge of renewable energy, automation, electrical design, energy management, research innovation, and community-based engineering solutions.

Professional development

I realized that effective electrical engineers need critical thinking, adaptability, communication skills, professionalism, and resilience.

Innovation mindset

The seminars inspired me to appreciate continuous learning, innovation, and research in advancing engineering practice.

Social responsibility

I became more motivated to use electrical engineering to contribute to sustainability, public service, and community improvement.
